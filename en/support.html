Ghostform Support

For help with the app or a purchase, email ghostform.support@gmail.com. Include your device model, the version shown on the home screen, and what happened.

Compare two clips

Choose Compare videos on the home screen. Add a reference clip, then your practice clip. Scrub each clip to the same moment, such as takeoff or impact. Switch between the overlay and side-by-side views to check what changed.

Watch the rep you just finished

Open Delayed replay, allow camera access, and place the phone where it can see your movement. Choose a delay long enough to finish the rep and turn toward the screen. If the camera stays dark, check Ghostform’s camera permission in your phone’s settings.

Find an exported video

After the export finishes, use the save button to put a copy in Photos on iPhone or your gallery on Android. Saving a comparison to Practice history keeps it inside Ghostform.

Restore PRO on a new phone

Sign in to the same store with the account that bought PRO. Open PRO in Ghostform and tap Restore purchases. The App Store and Google Play keep separate purchase records. If payment or family approval is pending, complete it through the store, then reopen Ghostform.

Price and refunds

PRO is a one-time purchase. The store shows the price for your region before you confirm. To request a refund, use Apple’s refund service or follow the Google Play refund instructions for the store where you bought PRO. Eligibility depends on your location and the circumstances of the purchase. For help finding your purchase, email Ghostform support.

Change ad privacy settings

Where available, select Ad privacy choices on the home screen. You can also manage tracking permission in iPhone Settings or your advertising ID in Android Settings.
