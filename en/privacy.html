Privacy policy

Updated November 26, 2026

Ghostform lets you compare practice videos and replay your movement on a delay. You can use the app without creating an account. The developer does not receive practice videos, pose landmarks, or app usage analytics.

Camera, video, and pose analysis

Camera frames, imported clips, and pose analysis are processed on your device. Ghostform uses pose landmarks to display the skeleton and align clips. Ghostform’s practice camera does not record microphone audio. On iPhone, a video recorded in the system video picker may include audio.

You choose whether Ghostform can access your camera or clips. Delayed replay needs camera access. Saving to Photos or your Android gallery creates a separate copy. Sharing passes the exported video to the app you select. Your device and the receiving app control any backup or further sharing.

Advertising and privacy choices

If your home screen has Ad settings, ads are off by default. If you are 18 or older, you can allow non-personalized ads there and turn them off again at any time. Your choice is saved on your device. You can compare videos, use delayed replay, and restore PRO with ads off.

The free version uses Google AdMob. When ads run, Google may receive a device advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location, device and diagnostic information, and ad interactions. Google uses these for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. In versions without Ad settings, ads may also be personalized where local rules and your consent settings allow it. PRO removes ads.

Where available, open Ad privacy choices on the home screen to review or change a choice. On iPhone you can change tracking permission in Settings under Privacy & Security. On Android you can manage your advertising ID in Settings. Read how Google uses information from apps and Google’s privacy policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the ad consent screen includes a list of Google’s advertising partners. Open the list to see which data each partner may use and for what purposes. You can decline consent or manage individual choices, including objections to processing based on legitimate interests. Ghostform does not request permission to access your device’s location or send GPS coordinates to advertisers. An IP address can indicate your approximate location.

Age information and family approval

Ghostform is designed for athletes and coaches and is not directed primarily to children under 13. In iOS 1.8 and Android 1.15, when the store indicates that an age check applies, the app requests an age range from the store. It uses the response in memory to decide whether ads may run. Ghostform does not ask for a birth date or identity document, save the age range, or send it to an advertiser. Practice features remain available if the check cannot be completed. Parents or guardians should approve a child’s use and recording.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes PRO payments and applicable family approvals. Ghostform checks the store’s purchase record to unlock or restore PRO. The developer does not receive payment card details. See Apple’s privacy policy or Google’s privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Practice history, local clips, and settings are stored on your device. Delete a saved session in Practice history. Removing the app deletes its local data. Delete exported copies separately in Photos or your gallery, and manage backups through your device.

Official website and browser demo

The measured pages of the official website and browser demo count visits, completed sample interactions, and store link clicks. A random visit ID in session storage expires after 30 minutes. The measurement database does not store videos, filenames, email addresses, IP addresses, user agents, or referring URLs. It uses Cloudflare and removes rows daily after 90 days of inactivity. Aggregate counts are used for private reports. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are respected. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy or change website measurement settings on this browser.

Where your information is handled

If you use Ghostform in Québec, some information may be handled outside Québec or Canada. Apple or Google processes the transaction when you buy PRO. When ads run, Google may process the advertising data described above on servers in other countries. If you email support, Gmail handles the message and the developer answers from Japan. You can describe a problem without attaching a practice video.

Questions and changes

For questions about personal data, or to request access or a correction, contact Ghostform’s Data Protection Officer, Riku Hirata, at ghostform.support@gmail.com. The developer receives the email address and information you choose to send. Updates to this policy appear on this page.
